TERRAIN ACCESS SURVEY v0.4.2 — RUNTIME MESH PILOT

UPDATING YOUR RUN
1. In game: /terrainsurvey stop
   Then: /lua stop TerrainSurvey
2. Extract the entire Windows ZIP. Double-click Install.cmd.
3. Confirm your RynthAi MetaFiles folder, RynthLua Scripts folder and the new
   TerrainMesh companion folder. Custom folders and other drives work.
4. ONCE: RynthCore > Plugins > Add Plugin. Select the companion DLL shown by
   the installer (its full path is copied to your clipboard). Enable
   ACAI2.Terrain, RynthAi and RynthNav. This adds OUR plugin, not a Rynth DLL patch.
5. In game:
   /lua start TerrainSurvey
   /lua autostart TerrainSurvey character
   /terrainsurvey resume

Use Resume to keep your existing inspection results and checkpoint. Only a
first-ever run, or an intentional replacement of the old run, uses:
/terrainsurvey new

WHAT CHANGED IN 0.4.2
Dense respawns no longer keep the survey waiting for an empty radar.
While walking inspection routes, the survey uses native Boost Nav Priority
for 15 seconds of actual movement, then releases navigation for up to 30
seconds of normal fighting and looting. This window ends early after at
least 3 seconds when targets and nearby loot work clear. A blocked travel
attempt with nearby targets also releases navigation to fight before retrying.

Rynth still owns healing, buffs, combat, looting and movement. Healing and
buffs take priority over boosted navigation. Fight/loot windows are bounded:
a fresh pack does not keep resetting the wait. Ordinary loot remains subject
to your loot setting and profile; a window does not promise every corpse.
Your original Boost Nav Priority setting is saved and restored on Stop.

Local return travel, bounded final recall, accepted mesh geometry and live
position diagnostics from 0.4.1 are retained. Status now shows the current
travel or fight_loot cycle. Mesh idle during a fight window is expected.

Resource housekeeping: eight rotating 512-Ki-character log slots (about 4 MiB
ASCII; at most about 16 MiB of UTF-8), maximum 256 corners per accepted route,
two temporary nav files per character and no growing in-memory route history.
Routine checkpoints are once per minute; progress, final recall intent,
recovery changes and log rotation are saved immediately. Repetitive ordinary
loot messages are counted in minute summaries instead of logged individually.
The companion does not maintain its own mesh-tile cache or increase Rynth's
25-tile request limit. Whole-client memory and cumulative CPU are sampled once/minute for analysis.
These optional samples do not force GC or automatically stop the client. They
include all apps in the process, so they cannot isolate survey resource use.

If already installed: update the COMPLETE package, then reload ACAI2.Terrain
through RynthCore Plugins, or restart that client, so the new DLL is loaded.
The Lua helper requires companion protocol 2, also used by 0.4.1. Missing or
incompatible companions stop clearly instead of walking without diagnostics.

RUNTIME MESH BEHAVIOR
The original survey followed fixed waypoints prepared from mesh data. This
version asks your installed RynthNav to plan actual walking steps from your
live position, using the intended target elevation. RynthAi follows the checked
corners and remains responsible for combat, buffing, looting and corpse recovery.
The companion never presses movement keys or starts its own walker.

Every accepted step must have a complete mesh path, matching starting/ending
ground layers, clear mesh raycasts and at least 15 yards of clearance from our
known camp portal neighborhoods. Partial paths and silent straight fallback
are refused. RynthNav's portal avoidance is also enabled with a minimum of
10 metres; larger existing settings are preserved. Native wall avoidance is
requested per path without changing your saved wall settings.

The survey chooses successive goals along the planned terrain corridor,
usually at least 40 yards ahead, using existing terrain vertices and their
known elevations. RynthNav plans the corners between those goals at runtime.
It does not plan several times per second: the companion checks the active
mesh segment up to four times per second, and replans at the next goal, after
combat displacement, on a blocked raycast, or after 12 seconds without progress.

For a snag it tries a fresh mesh-checked approach via the left and right at
10 yards, then left and right at 18 yards. Each one must still pass all checks.
After these attempts and the travel budget, existing hub recovery/retry or
point deferral continues. Deferring a point never proves the terrain impassable.

A mesh cannot guarantee every rock or changing object is modeled. The bounded
side approaches and logs address that gap. This is an unverified live pilot;
controlled tests and real mesh-data checks do not replace an in-game test.

WHAT IT EXPLORES
36 cliff/elevation approaches, using /ls to the 275 Plateau hub and the known
180/225 portals. The intended order is 275, 180, then 225. These are new terrain
inspection corridors, not the old hunting circuits. No quest NPCs or treasure
maps are used. The data is intended to improve future treasure-map routing.

YOUR SETTINGS AND RECOVERY
Ordinary Enable Looting is preserved. You may leave it off in dense packs.
It is temporarily enabled only for native own-corpse recovery, then restored.
Buffs, death/recovery and portal transitions pause survey travel. Native busy
actions pause progress checks; Rynth handles their priority. Combat candidates
do not block a walking burst. Fight/loot windows temporarily disable navigation.
HP fluctuations from buffs do not count as death; zero HP does.

Native RynthAi must remember the death to find its corpse. The survey waits
for native recovery and then continues the unfinished point. Your Rynth
launcher handles auto-relog; the Lua autostart above restores the recorder.
A missing recorder is automatically restarted with a bounded retry budget.
Explicit Stop is respected. Missing required plugins, persistent disk failure,
another controller or exhausted recovery retries can stop with progress saved.

CONTROLS
/terrainsurvey status  — version, state, mesh phase, wait reason and log folder
/terrainsurvey stop    — stop and retain progress
/terrainsurvey resume  — continue the saved run
/terrainsurvey new     — replace this character's run and diagnostic ring
/lua execin TerrainSurvey chat(TerrainSurveyVersion or 'older')

WHERE YOUR FILES GO
Meta: your chosen RynthAi\MetaFiles\Terrain Access Survey.af
Lua:  your chosen RynthLua\Scripts\TerrainSurvey\index.lua and two JSON files
Companion: your chosen folder\ACAI2.Rynth.Terrain.Plugin.dll and dependencies
Logs/checkpoint: your actual RynthLua\Data\TerrainSurvey folder
Temporary walks: active RynthAi\NavProfiles\ACAI2-Terrain (two files/character)

Your existing Data folder is preserved. The installer backs up replaced survey
and companion files under LocalAppData\ACAI2-TerrainSurvey\Backups. It never
replaces RynthAi, RynthNav, RynthLua, loot profiles or character runtime data.

Logs rotate through eight bounded files. Samples are every five seconds plus
state/outcome events. Ordinary chat is excluded. Results are written to disk
before the next point is credited. A completed pass ends after the bounded final hub-return attempt. The
14-hour wall-clock limit can also end it with progress saved. Zip the Data\TerrainSurvey folder afterward for analysis;
it is not uploaded automatically. Do not publish player logs to the public repo.

MANUAL INSTALL
Copy MetaFiles\Terrain Access Survey.af into the active MetaFiles folder.
Copy the whole Scripts\TerrainSurvey folder into RynthLua\Scripts.
Copy the whole Plugin folder into a dedicated companion installation folder;
add its ACAI2.Rynth.Terrain.Plugin.dll through RynthCore Plugins once.
Do not copy the DLL into RynthAi's folder. Optional NavProfiles files are
fixed-path previews only; the actual survey uses checked runtime mesh steps.
