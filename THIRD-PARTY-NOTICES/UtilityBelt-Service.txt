ACAI2's minimal native attack-power binding derives the requested-power field
layout, getter/UI-notification entrypoints and call-site map from these files.
The x86 layout expands the source's ClientSystem, IInputActionCallback,
QualityChangeHandler and Turbine_RefCount bases: mode=0x1C, pending=0x20,
height=0x24, m_rUIRequestedPower=0x4C. No UtilityBelt binary is bundled or required.
